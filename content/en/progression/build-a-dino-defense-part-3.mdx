## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most common mistake during build a dino defense part 3?
The most frequent mistake is over-expanding the perimeter before establishing automated ammunition supplies. Players frequently construct massive walls that dilute turret density, allowing elite dinosaurs to isolate and breach unguarded sections.

### Can tamed dinosaurs completely replace automated turrets?
While trained dinosaurs provide high sustain and do not consume ammunition, they are vulnerable to status effects like bleeding, venom, and armor-shredding debuffs. The ideal setup combines automated turrets for consistent ranged damage with durable dinosaurs acting as front-line anchors.

### How do I protect anti-air turrets from being targeted first?
Enclose aerial defense batteries behind half-walls or grated roofs that provide vertical line of sight while shielding the equipment from ground-based charges and low-altitude spitters.

### How often should defensive layouts be audited?
Audit your compound every five to ten waves. As enemy AI levels increase, dinosaurs will locate the weakest points in your pathfinding corridors, requiring regular trap relocation and wall reinforcements.
