## Input Troubleshooting and Control Optimization

### Troubleshooting Common Control Glitches

1. **Stuck Bottom Interface:** If the lower card hand freezes or refuses to register swipes, verify that full-screen touch gestures on your device are not conflicting with in-game borders.
2. **Accidental Drops:** Sweaty fingers or high touch sensitivity can drop cards onto unintended tiles. Enable "Confirm Summon" in your settings if available, or clean your display surface.
3. **Power Display Sync:** In older builds, battle power numbers occasionally lagged behind real-time stats. Ensure your game is updated to the latest build to keep card levels accurately synced with live combat values.

| Issue Encountered | Probable Cause | Quick Fix Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Card Drag Lag** | Low device memory or battery saver throttling | Lower graphics quality; disable background battery savers |
| **Summon Misclick** | Cluttered touch zones near edge borders | Adjust in-game HUD scaling or use stylus/virtual controls |
| **Merge Failure** | Dragging incompatible unit tiers | Check star tiers; cards must be identical levels to merge |
