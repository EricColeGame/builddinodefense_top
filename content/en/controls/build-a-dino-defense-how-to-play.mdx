## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Play and Deploy Units

Learning **build a dino defense how to play** efficiently comes down to executing a steady loop of resource generation, card draws, and board positioning. Mismanaging your deployment bench during the early seconds of a stage often causes leaks that snowball into defeat.

```
[Generate Energy] ➔ [Draw Dino Card] ➔ [Merge Duplicates] ➔ [Deploy to Lane] ➔ [Trigger Skills]
```

### 1. Generating Deployment Resources
Battles begin with a baseline reserve of energy. Defeating minor minions refunds small increments of power, allowing you to sustain unit summons. Avoid spending all your resources instantly; keep enough energy banked to react if a swift flyer or armored beast charges your defense.

### 2. Summoning from Your Deck
Tapping the summon icon costs a flat energy fee that scales up slightly with each summon in a single wave cycle. Cards drawn land directly on your lower staging area. 

### 3. Executing the Merge Mechanic
When two cards share identical species and star ranks, dragging one onto the other combines them into an upgraded variant. Upgraded dinos gain increased health, attack power, and frequently unlock passive aura buffs that benefit adjacent units.

### 4. Directing Units onto the Field
Once satisfied with your unit’s tier, swipe the card upward into the designated defensive perimeter. Once deployed into the combat path, dinosaurs automatically engage oncoming foes within their aggro radius.

## Advanced Battle Tactics and Wave Management

Once you understand the basic controls, surviving endless or nightmare stages requires tactical foresight. As enemy waves increase in density, brute-force summoning ceases to be effective. Incorporate these battle-tested strategies into your defense routine:

### Lane Staggering and Chokepoint Creation
Never spread your forces evenly across every path if the map features diverging lanes. Identify the intersection where creeps merge and concentrate your high-durability tanks there. This clusters enemies together, drastically multiplying the effectiveness of your ranged splash-damage attacks.

### Managing the "Merge Bait" Trap
A frequent beginner mistake is instantly merging every duplicate unit the second it appears. Merging two Level 1 units creates a single Level 2 unit. While the Level 2 dinosaur possesses stronger individual stats, two Level 1 units often deal more cumulative attacks per second against sprawling swarms. Only merge when:
- Your bench is full and preventing new draws.
- A high-armor elite enemy demands single-target armor penetration.
- You have surplus energy ready to summon a replacement unit immediately.

### Timing Ability Bursts for Boss Encounters
Boss enemies possess barrier mechanics that absorb standard physical attacks. Monitor boss travel paths and hold your manual tactical strikes until their support minions are cleared, ensuring your high-impact damage lands squarely on the primary target.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important skill in build a dino defense how to play?
Resource discipline is the most critical skill. Players who spend all their summon energy the moment it becomes available cannot react when fast runner units exploit open lanes. Always keep a reserve buffer to drop immediate counters.

### How do I unlock higher-tier dinosaurs?
Higher-tier cards are unlocked by clearing milestone campaign stages, completing daily quests, and merging duplicate cards collected from battle reward chests. Upgrading cards in your permanent collection raises their starting stats on the battlefield.

### Should I prioritize merging or board presence?
In the early waves, prioritize board presence. Having multiple units attacking simultaneously clears early waves faster than having one high-tier unit with a slow attack animation. Transition to merging once your bench fills up or elite monsters begin spawning.

### How does this guide on build a dino defense how to play help with boss stages?
Boss stages require saving burst abilities, building specialized armor-shredding ranged units, and funneling the boss into a single lane using durable frontline tanks. This guide provides the class balances and deck ratios required to sustain the fight against massive single-target health pools.
