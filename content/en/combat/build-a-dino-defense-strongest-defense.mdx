## Card Merging and Upgrade Economics

Success in wave defense games depends heavily on how efficiently you combine duplicate cards. Merging cards does not simply provide linear stat increases—it fundamentally alters survival thresholds. Upgrading a card doubles its resilience, unlocks secondary passives, and optimizes grid real estate.

### Merge Priority Ranking

1. **Main Tank:** If your frontline collapses, backline damage is irrelevant. Always upgrade your primary wall first.
2. **AoE DPS:** Cleaving down weak minions keeps your single-target attackers focused on priority targets.
3. **Control Units:** Enhancing CC units increases stun durations and freeze chances.
4. **Secondary Fillers:** Reserve merges for auxiliary units only after your primary carry reaches critical milestones.

## Tactical Combat Tips for Pushing Late Waves

Fielding strong units is only half the battle; real-time execution determines whether your line holds or collapses.

1. **Stagger Your Summons:** Never spend all your deployment points at once. If an elite wave immediately drops an AoE ground hazard, you will lose your entire investment.
2. **Track Enemy Resistances:** Certain wave modifiers grant temporary resistance to physical knockbacks. When these enemies appear, lean into elemental dot damage like poison or burn.
3. **Protect Your Win Conditions:** If your primary damage dealer is a ranged backliner, ensure enemy burrowers or flying units cannot bypass your frontline tanks.
4. **Time Board Clears:** Save your high-cooldown board skills until the boss summons auxiliary waves. Wiping out the minion wave immediately redirects your heavy hitters back to the primary boss.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important card when trying to build a dino defense strongest defense?
While offensive damage finishes stages, the frontline tank—specifically an armored unit like the Ankylosaur—is the undisputed foundation. Without a high-durability anchor to group enemies together, your ranged DPS units will be wiped out before dealing meaningful damage.

### Should I prioritize merging cards immediately or filling all field slots?
In the early game, filling all active grid slots is generally better to establish baseline damage output. Once all positions are populated, prioritize merging low-tier duplicates into Tier 2 and Tier 3 units to concentrate power and unlock essential passives.

### How do I stop fast enemy rushers from breaking through my frontline?
Incorporate crowd-control specialists that deal knockback or slowing effects. Placing units like Triceratops or ice-based spitters just behind your main tank will continually disrupt rushing enemies and keep them trapped in your kill zone.
