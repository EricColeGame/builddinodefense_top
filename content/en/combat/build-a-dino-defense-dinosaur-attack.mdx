1. **Frontline Collision:** Halting advancing mobs before they approach projectile range of your nest.

## Optimal Build Strategies for Early, Mid, and Late Game

Your operational approach must evolve as stages grow increasingly brutal. Early challenges feature straightforward infantry charges, but advanced sectors introduce high-damage siege tools, shielded primitive bosses, and rapid-assault runners.

### Early Game: Low-Cost Momentum (Stages 1–25)

During early progression, prioritize low-cost deployment cycles. Your goal is to establish field presence before the opponent establishes lane control.

1. Spawn 1–2 light interceptors immediately to contest the center field.
2. Drop an armored tank once initial energy accumulates.
3. Conserve secondary weapons (such as basic rock drops) exclusively for emergency nest defense.

### Mid Game: Defensive Fortification (Stages 26–60)

Mid-tier levels test your ability to juggle crowd control with continuous unit replenishment. Here, cavemen begin deploying high-damage throwing weapons.

* Maintain at least two Ankylosaurus units on the field to stagger frontline impact.
* Pair each tank wave with trailing raptor brawlers to deal continuous DPS while shielded.
* Pre-place hidden ground snares at choke points to eliminate sprinters attempting to bypass your heavy units.

### Late Game: The Titan Assault (Stages 61–100+)

High-tier stages feature massive enemy density capable of melting standard tanks in seconds. You cannot survive via passive defense; you must mount an overwhelming counter-offensive.

* Open with heavy disruption (Blizzards or sustained Ice Rocks) to neutralize the opening rush.
* Funnel incoming waves into tight kill boxes using Decoy Eggs.
* Deploy an Ankylosaurus, immediately followed by a Triceratops and Tyrannosaurus, creating an armored rolling juggernaut that steadily clears the screen.

## Advanced Combat Tactics for Overcoming Boss Waves

Boss encounters introduce towering primitive warlords equipped with massive health pools and devastating area-of-effect swings. Meeting a boss head-on without tactical preparation will wipe your field clean.

Implement these three advanced maneuvers to take down boss waves without sustaining critical nest damage:

### 1. The Staggered Stun Lock

Never cast all crowd-control abilities simultaneously. Drop an ice rock to freeze the boss, allow your T-Rex to land two solid bites, then deploy a secondary stun (such as an explosive blast or sidekick attack) just as the freeze effect expires. This maximizes downtime and minimizes incoming damage.

### 2. The Decoy Redirect

When a boss prepares an overhead slam, drop a Decoy Egg behind the boss unit if lane positioning allows, or slightly off-axis. Many heavy enemies will pivot their attack trajectory toward the decoy, sparing your primary melee frontliners from critical damage.

### 3. Wave Stacking Behind the Egg

If the enemy push becomes overwhelming, halt forward summoning and let the frontline drift closer to your base defenses. Once the invaders pack tightly together directly outside your base perimeter, unleash an emergency lane-clearing spell (like a Volcano Bomb) followed immediately by a rapid double-tank spawn. This tactic completely resets field momentum in your favor.

### When should I deploy active spells versus summoning dinosaurs?
Save active spells for high-density enemy clusters or emergency counters against rapid infiltrators. Deploying a high-cost spell against a few isolated infantry wastes resources; instead, rely on your basic units to hold standard lanes, reserving high-impact magic to shatter concentrated assault waves.

### Can fast units like Velociraptors serve as primary defenders?
No. Velociraptors lack the defensive stats required to hold a lane. They excel as rapid-response flanking units, finishing off wounded enemies, or eliminating backline primitive archers. Always pair them with heavy frontline tanks to ensure they survive long enough to deal damage.
