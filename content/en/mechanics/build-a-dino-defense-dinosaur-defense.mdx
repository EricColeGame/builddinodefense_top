## Core Mechanics: Merging, Deck Building, and Energy Economy

At its foundation, this style of tactical defense combines deck building with on-the-fly merging mechanics. Rather than placing static turrets, you command a dynamic deck of prehistoric cards. During combat, energy accumulates over time, enabling you to summon dinosaurs onto your defensive line.

When two duplicate cards of the same rank share the field or hand, you can merge them into a higher-tier unit. Merging boosts core attributes such as attack power, attack speed, and health pools, often unlocking secondary combat perks.

### The Combat Loop Breakdown

```
Draw Cards ➔ Evaluate Energy ➔ Summon Tactical Units ➔ Merge Duplicates ➔ Trigger Skills ➔ Clear Waves
```

Managing your summon economy is critical. Summons scale in cost, meaning reckless deployment quickly leaves you resource-starved when elite threats breach your perimeter. A balanced deck balances low-cost swarm blockers with high-impact, high-cost apex predators.

| Resource Element | Primary Source | Strategic Purpose | Common Mistake |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most effective way to build a dino defense dinosaur defense squad for beginners?
The most reliable beginner strategy is the "2-2-1 Rule": run two frontline tanks, two long-range AoE siege cards, and one high-single-target DPS unit. This structure offers a resilient defensive wall while delivering enough splash damage to manage swarms and targeted power to clear early bosses.

### Should I merge my dinosaurs immediately during a wave?
Not always. Merging removes one unit from the board to create a stronger one. If you merge while a large enemy wave is actively engaging your line, your overall damage per second may temporarily drop, risking a perimeter breach. Clear the immediate pressure first, or wait for the lull between waves to execute higher-tier merges.

### How do I counter high-speed flying enemies?
Flying or rapid enemies bypass slow frontline tanks. To counter them, place rapid-fire skirmishers in your midline and backline. Combining skirmishers with slowing auras ensures high-speed threats are eliminated before they reach your base gates.

### Which card upgrades provide the highest return on investment?
Upgrading energy-generation abilities and primary AoE artillery cards offers the best early-to-mid-game value. AoE units scale exceptionally well because every attack stat increase multiplies across every enemy caught in their blast radius.
