### 1. The Opening Engagement (Phase 1)
Open the fight by leading with your Tanker to absorb the initial charge attack. Do not burn your primary offensive ultimates immediately. Instead, rotate through your utility abilities to apply defense-down debuffs and trigger your Amplifier's teamwide damage boosts.

### 2. Creating the Stagger Window
Once the boss reaches maximum stagger buildup from heavy impacts, a brief vulnerability window opens. Swap directly to your main carry, unleash your full ability rotation, and bring the boss below the 50% health threshold before it recovers.

### 3. Navigating Phase 2 Enrage
At half health, the build a dino defense third boss emits an arena-wide shockwave. Immediately switch back to your defensive or mobility options. The boss's attack speed increases significantly during this phase, making standing still fatal. Keep your distance, dodge cleaves, re-apply your shred abilities, and chip away at the remaining health bar until your ultimates are ready for the final burst execution.
